# Musi Riverfront Explorer

An interactive look at what the Musi Riverfront tender drawings show for Zone 1A and Zone 1B in Hyderabad. Swipe between today and the plan, then step through the stretches.

**Live:** https://dimoai25.github.io/musi-riverfront-explorer/

This is an approximate overlay based on public plans. It is not for judging impact on any specific property or land acquisition, and it is not official or investment advice. Made by [@Vamsidhar_t](https://x.com/Vamsidhar_t).

Sources: the Zone 1B construction tender documents (contract 07/CE/MRDCL/2026-27) on the Central Public Procurement Portal, mainly master plan sheet 1 (MHT-MP-PP-01-Z1, Rev R02, Sep 2026) and the Zone 1 design basis reports. Each stretch in the explorer lists its exact sources.
